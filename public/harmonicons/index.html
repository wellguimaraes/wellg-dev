Harmonicons

by WellG Tech Co.

A calm puzzle game about symmetry. Swap icons to build mirrored rows, palindromes, diagonals and symmetric boards. New patterns unlock as you climb.

Frequently asked

  • How do I replay the tutorial? Tap “How to play” on the home screen.
  • Does the game need an internet connection? No. Harmonicons works fully offline.
  • Can I change the language? Yes. Pick English, Português or Русский at the bottom of the home screen, or leave it on Auto to follow your device.
  • Is my progress saved? Your language choice and the lessons you have seen are saved on your device.
